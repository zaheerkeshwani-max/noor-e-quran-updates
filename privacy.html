Noor-e-Quran: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Noor-e-Quran is a Quran reading app made by ZK Graphics (Zaheer Keshwani). This page explains what the app does with your information.

We do not collect personal data

The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. Your bookmarks, settings, reading progress and downloaded audio stay on your phone. We do not receive them.

What the app downloads

  • Tafseer: when you open the tafseer of an ayah for the first time, the app fetches it from tafsir.majmuo.com and keeps a copy on the phone.
  • Recitation audio: when you download a surah's audio, the app fetches the files from Google Drive.
  • Corrections: the app checks this website (zaheerkeshwani-max.github.io) now and then for corrected text and pages, and downloads them.

These sites see an ordinary web request from your phone (such as its IP address), as with any website. The app sends them no personal information.

Reporting a problem

If you use “Report a Problem”, the app opens your own e-mail app with a message to the developer and, if you choose, a screenshot. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your e-mail app. We use these e-mails only to fix the app.

Voice search and notifications

Voice search uses your phone's own speech recognizer; the app does not record audio itself. The daily ayah notification is made on the phone.

Backups

A backup file is saved only where you choose on your phone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
